# Recorded demo foley

Both sources are studio recordings by Joseph SARDIN, released under CC0.
Downloaded from their normal WAV download forms on 2026-09-29.

- `magic-mouse.wav`: [Apple Magic Mouse, Single Click](https://bigsoundbank.com/apple-magic-mouse-simple-clic-s1742.html), 48 kHz / 24-bit mono.
- `imac-keyboard.wav`: [Quick iMac Keyboard](https://bigsoundbank.com/clavier-imac-rapide-s1731.html), 48 kHz / 24-bit mono.

The soundtrack trims silence, lightly filters rumble/high frequencies, compresses
keyboard transients for consistent levels, and places the recordings at captured interaction times. Keyboard
bursts follow human prompt entry, with no simulated key sounds over AI output
or notification-reading holds. No music, voiceover, or synthesized tones.

The silent master remains unchanged. The earlier synthesized soundtrack is
retained only in the legacy reproduction script, not used in this export.
